# TBT-075 Mobile Phones and Driving

## Why It Matters
Mobile Phones and Driving matters because driving for work carries some of the highest risk exposure of any task we do, and fatigue, distraction and vehicle condition all contribute.

## KEY HAZARDS
- Collision due to distraction or fatigue
- poor vehicle condition
- adverse weather
- unsecured loads shifting in transit

## REQUIRED CONTROLS
- Carry out vehicle daily checks before driving
- plan journeys to allow adequate rest breaks
- secure all loads before moving off
- adjust driving to weather and road conditions

## EMPLOYEES MUST
- Complete a daily vehicle check before driving
- wear a seatbelt at all times
- take breaks on long journeys
- secure tools, cylinders and materials before travelling

## EMPLOYEES MUST NOT
- Use a hand-held phone while driving
- drive when fatigued or unfit to do so
- carry unsecured loads or exceed the vehicle's payload

## GOOD PRACTICE
- Plan routes and breaks before setting off
- report vehicle defects promptly
- keep the vehicle tidy so loose items can't become hazards in a collision

## EMERGENCY / INCIDENT RESPONSE
- Stop in a safe place and call for help if unwell or fatigued
- report a collision immediately, whether or not there is damage or injury
- follow the company accident procedure and exchange details as required
