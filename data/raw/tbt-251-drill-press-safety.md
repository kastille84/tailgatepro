# TBT-251 Drill Press Safety

## Why It Matters

Drill Press Safety matters because contact with rotating or moving machinery parts can cause severe entanglement or crush injuries in a fraction of a second.

## KEY HAZARDS

- Entanglement in rotating parts (fans, belts, couplings)
- crush injuries
- unexpected start-up while working on equipment
- loose clothing or hair caught in machinery

## REQUIRED CONTROLS

- Isolate and lock off before working on or near rotating equipment
- ensure guards are fitted and in good condition before starting
- remove loose clothing, jewellery and tie back long hair
- never bypass an interlock

## EMPLOYEES MUST

- Isolate and lock off equipment before removing guards
- confirm guards are refitted and secure before restarting
- report missing, damaged or defeated guards immediately
- keep hands and tools clear of moving parts

## EMPLOYEES MUST NOT

- Operate equipment with guards removed or interlocks defeated
- reach into or near moving parts
- wear loose clothing, gloves or jewellery near rotating machinery

## GOOD PRACTICE

- Check guards as part of every pre-use inspection
- keep the area around rotating equipment clear and tidy
- report unusual noise or vibration promptly

## EMERGENCY / INCIDENT RESPONSE

- Use the emergency stop immediately
- isolate the power supply
- do not attempt to free a person from moving machinery — stop the machine first
- call for first aid/emergency services
