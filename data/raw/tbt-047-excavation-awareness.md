# TBT-047 Excavation Awareness

## Why It Matters
Excavation Awareness matters because ground and temporary works hazards are often hidden until something fails, so planning and verification before work starts is essential.

## KEY HAZARDS
- Collapse of excavation sides
- contact with underground/buried services
- instability of temporary structures
- falls into excavations

## REQUIRED CONTROLS
- Obtain and check service drawings/CAT scan before excavating
- support or batten excavations as required
- barrier and sign excavations
- have temporary works designs checked and approved before loading

## EMPLOYEES MUST
- Locate and mark services before breaking ground
- use a CAT scanner and safe digging practices
- barrier excavations and cover or fence them when unattended
- get temporary works approved before use

## EMPLOYEES MUST NOT
- Excavate without checking for buried services first
- enter an unsupported excavation over 1.2m without assessment
- load temporary works beyond their design capacity

## GOOD PRACTICE
- Keep spoil away from the excavation edge
- inspect excavation supports daily and after weather events
- record permit-to-dig checks

## EMERGENCY / INCIDENT RESPONSE
- Stop work immediately if a service is struck
- evacuate the area for gas or electrical strikes and call the relevant utility
- report the incident and do not attempt repairs yourself

## Supervisor Discussion
- What are the main hazards of Excavation Awareness on our jobs, and which of today's controls matters most on this site?
- Which of these controls is easiest to skip when we're under time pressure — and what's the cost if it goes wrong?
- What would make you stop the job and ask for help?
