# Protecting Yourself When Handling Contaminated Sharps (OSHA FactSheet)

FactSheet

Protecting Yourself When Handling
Contaminated Sharps

Sharps are objects that can penetrate a worker's skin, such as needles, scalpels,
broken glass, capillary tubes and the exposed ends of dental wires. If blood or
other potentially infectious materials (OPIM), as defined in the OSHA Bloodborne
Pathogens standard (29 CFR 1910.1030), are present or may be present on the
sharp, it is a contaminated sharp and appropriate personal protective equipment
must be worn.
