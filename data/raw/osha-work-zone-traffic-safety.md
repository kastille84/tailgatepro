# Work Zone Traffic Safety (FactSheet)

Work Zone Traffic Safety

Transportation incidents and workers struck by vehicles or mobile equipment
account for the highest number of fatal work injuries, according to the Bureau of
Labor Statistics. Workers such as emergency responders, clean-up, utility, demo-
lition, construction, and others in areas where there are moving vehicles and traf-
fic are exposed to being struck-by moving vehicles. Work zones are used to move
traffic in an approved direction and are typically identified by signs, cones, bar-
rels, and barriers.
