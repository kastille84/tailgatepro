                Lead Exposure

    Never blast, sand, or cut with a torch any surfaces that may contain lead until it has been

          tested for lead.

    Wash your hands and face before eating, drinking, or using tobacco.
    Do not eat, drink, or use tobacco products in the work area.
    Use a vacuum attachment, follow product instructions, and wear PPE when you might

          come into contact with lead.
