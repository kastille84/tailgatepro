# Cold and Work: Types, Causes, Preparation

Workers exposed to extreme cold or cold environments may experience cold stress and be at risk for cold-related illness: hypothermia, frostbite, trench foot, chilblains. Cold stress occurs when body heat is lost rapidly. At risk: outdoor workers, those in poorly insulated spaces, those without shelter.

## Employer measures
Schedule cold tasks for warmer periods; rotate workers; provide heated beverages and warm break areas; monitor vulnerable workers; train on risks, prevention, symptoms, monitoring, treatment, protective equipment.

## Worker measures
Layered loose clothing; waterproof insulated boots and hat; breaks in warm locations; carry backup clothing, blankets, thermometers, chemical heat packs; avoid bare skin on cold metal; watch yourself and coworkers.

Related NIOSH note (archive.cdc.gov update): workers in cold storage, food processing, and supermarkets may spend most of a shift in refrigerated rooms.
