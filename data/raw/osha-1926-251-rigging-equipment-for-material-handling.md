# 1926.251 Rigging equipment for material handling

(a) General. Rigging equipment must be inspected before each shift and during use. Equipment must have permanently affixed and legible identification markings as prescribed by the manufacturer showing safe working loads, and must not be loaded beyond those limits. Defective equipment must be removed from service immediately.
(b) Alloy steel chains. Chain slings need permanent identification markings (size, grade, rated capacity). Attachments must meet or exceed chain capacity. Job or shop hooks and links, or makeshift fasteners formed from bolts, rods, etc., are prohibited. Periodic inspections required at least annually. Wear limits in Table H-1 require removal from service when exceeded.
(c) Wire rope. Loads must not exceed rated capacity. Eye splices need not less than three full tucks. Wire rope cannot be secured by knots except on haul back lines. Slings with broken wires exceeding 10 percent of the total number of wires in any 8-diameter length must be removed. Shock loading and pulling slings from under loads are prohibited.
(d) Natural and synthetic fiber rope. Manila rope eye splices need three full tucks minimum; synthetic fiber requires four. Temperature range -20 F to +180 F without load reduction. Remove for abnormal wear, broken fibers, or discoloration.
(e) Synthetic webbing. Marked with manufacturer name, rated capacities, and material type. Stitching only for end fittings. Temperature limits 180 F for nylon/polyester; 200 F for polypropylene. Nylon cannot be used near acids; polyester/polypropylene forbidden near caustics. Remove if burnt, torn, or with broken stitches.
(f) Shackles and hooks. Must not exceed rated capacities. Hooks without manufacturer recommendations must be proof-tested to twice the intended safe working load.
