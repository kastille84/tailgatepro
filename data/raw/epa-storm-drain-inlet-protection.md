Applicability

Inlet protection is applicable to operational inlets for
which all or some of the inlet’s drainage area is
disturbed. Storm drain inlet protection is a secondary
control device, meaning that construction staff should
always use inlet protection in conjunction with other
sediment and erosion control practices.
