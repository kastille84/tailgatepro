# TBT-246 Charging Electric Vehicles

## Why It Matters
Charging Electric Vehicles matters because batteries store significant energy and can present fire, chemical and electrical hazards if damaged or charged incorrectly.

## KEY HAZARDS
- Fire or explosion from charging faults
- chemical burns from battery acid/electrolyte
- electric shock
- hydrogen gas build-up during charging

## REQUIRED CONTROLS
- Charge batteries in a ventilated, designated area away from ignition sources
- use the correct charger for the battery type
- inspect batteries and leads for damage before use
- keep naked flames and smoking materials away from charging areas

## EMPLOYEES MUST
- Charge batteries only in designated, ventilated areas
- use the correct charger and leads for the battery type
- report swollen, damaged or leaking batteries immediately
- wear appropriate PPE when handling battery acid/electrolyte

## EMPLOYEES MUST NOT
- Charge batteries in an unventilated or enclosed space
- use damaged batteries, chargers or leads
- smoke or allow naked flames near charging batteries

## GOOD PRACTICE
- Turn off chargers once charging is complete
- keep charging areas clean and free of combustible materials
- store batteries upright and secured

## EMERGENCY / INCIDENT RESPONSE
- Evacuate the area if a battery overheats, smokes or catches fire
- do not use water on a lithium battery fire — use the appropriate extinguisher or let trained responders handle it
- call the fire service for battery fires
