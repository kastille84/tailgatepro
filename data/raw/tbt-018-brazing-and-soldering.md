# TBT-018 Brazing and Soldering

## Why It Matters
Brazing and Soldering matters because hot work is a leading cause of fire on construction and industrial sites, often with a delayed ignition after the work has finished.

## KEY HAZARDS
- Fire from sparks, flame or heat
- burns
- fumes
- damage to nearby equipment/refrigerant lines
- explosion where flammable atmospheres are present

## REQUIRED CONTROLS
- Obtain a hot work permit before starting
- clear/protect combustible materials from the area
- keep a fire extinguisher and fire watch in place
- check for flammable atmospheres, especially near refrigerant

## EMPLOYEES MUST
- Hold a valid hot work permit before starting
- keep an extinguisher within reach and a fire watch during and after the work
- check the area is clear of flammable materials
- carry out the post-work fire check specified on the permit

## EMPLOYEES MUST NOT
- Start hot work without a permit where one is required
- leave hot work unattended
- carry out hot work near flammable refrigerant or gas without isolating/purging first
- remove the fire watch before the specified stand-down time

## GOOD PRACTICE
- Use screens to contain sparks
- keep the extinguisher rated for the fire risk present
- brief nearby trades before starting

## EMERGENCY / INCIDENT RESPONSE
- Raise the alarm if fire develops
- use the extinguisher only if safe and trained to do so
- evacuate the area
- call the fire service
