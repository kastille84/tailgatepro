# 1926.441 Batteries and battery charging

(a) General requirements. Unsealed batteries must be positioned in enclosed spaces with external vents or adequately ventilated areas to prevent fumes, gases, or electrolyte spray from entering other spaces. Ventilation systems should facilitate gas diffusion and prevent explosive mixture buildup. Battery storage racks and trays must be sturdy and treated to resist electrolyte damage. Flooring in battery areas requires acid-resistant construction or protective coating against acid accumulation. Workers handling acids or batteries must have access to face shields, aprons, and rubber gloves. "Facilities for quick drenching of the eyes and body shall be provided within 25 feet (7.62 m)" of battery handling zones. Spill response and fire suppression equipment must be available for electrolyte incidents.

(b) Charging. Battery charging operations must occur in designated locations only. Charging equipment needs protection from vehicle damage. "Vent caps shall be kept in place to avoid electrolyte spray" during charging, and these caps must remain in proper working condition.
