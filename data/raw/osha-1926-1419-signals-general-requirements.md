# 1926.1419 Signals - general requirements

(a) A signal person must be provided when: the point of operation is not in full view of the operator; when the equipment is traveling, the view in the direction of travel is obstructed; or site-specific safety concerns warrant one.
(b) Signals must be communicated through hand, voice, audible, or new signal methods.
(c) Hand signals must follow the Standard Method (Appendix A). Non-standard hand signals require prior agreement between the signal person, operator, and lift director.
(d) New signals are permissible if they provide at least equally effective communication as voice, audible, or Standard Method hand signals, or comply with applicable national consensus standards.
(e) Signal methods and transmission means must be appropriate for the site conditions.
(f) Signal transmission ability between operator and signal person must be maintained; operations must stop if interrupted.
(g) If the operator identifies a safety problem, operations must halt until resolution and mutual agreement.
(h) Only one person may signal at a time, with limited exceptions.
(j) Anyone aware of safety problems must alert via stop or emergency stop signal.
(k) Directions must be given from the operator's direction perspective.
(m) When communicating with multiple cranes/derricks, each signal must identify which equipment it applies to.
