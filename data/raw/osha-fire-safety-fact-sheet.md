Fire Safety

Employers should train workers about fire hazards in the workplace and about
what to do in a fire emergency. If you want your workers to evacuate, you should
train them on evacuation procedures. If you expect your workers to use fire
fighting equipment, you should provide the appropriate equipment and train
workers to use it safely. (See Title 29 of the Code of Federal Regulations (CFR)
Part 1910 Subparts E and L; and Part 1926 Subparts C and F.)
