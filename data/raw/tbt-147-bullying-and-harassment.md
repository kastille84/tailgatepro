# TBT-147 Bullying and Harassment

## Why It Matters
Bullying and Harassment matters because how we communicate, supervise and treat each other directly affects safety outcomes as much as any physical control.

## KEY HAZARDS
- Poor communication leading to misunderstandings
- inadequate supervision of new or inexperienced workers
- unacceptable behaviour undermining teamwork
- reluctance to raise concerns

## REQUIRED CONTROLS
- Brief tasks clearly and check understanding, especially with new starters
- provide appropriate supervision for the experience level of the team
- treat colleagues with respect and challenge poor behaviour
- encourage everyone to speak up about concerns

## EMPLOYEES MUST
- Ask if you are unsure about any instruction
- treat colleagues, clients and the public with respect
- support and supervise less experienced workers appropriately
- report bullying, harassment or unsafe behaviour

## EMPLOYEES MUST NOT
- Tolerate or ignore bullying, harassment or discriminatory behaviour
- leave new or inexperienced workers without adequate supervision
- carry out unfamiliar tasks without asking for guidance

## GOOD PRACTICE
- Take time to properly induct new and agency workers
- encourage questions rather than assuming understanding
- recognise good safety behaviour in others

## EMERGENCY / INCIDENT RESPONSE
- Report incidents of unacceptable behaviour to your supervisor or HR promptly
- seek support if you or a colleague is affected
- follow the company's reporting procedure

## Supervisor Discussion
- What are the main hazards of Bullying and Harassment on our jobs, and which of today's controls matters most on this site?
- Which of these controls is easiest to skip when we're under time pressure — and what's the cost if it goes wrong?
- What would make you stop the job and ask for help?
