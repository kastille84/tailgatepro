# TBT-117 Adhesives and Sealants

## Why It Matters
Adhesives and Sealants matters because chemical exposure can cause immediate injury or long-term ill health, and the controls needed depend on the specific substance.

## KEY HAZARDS
- Skin/eye contact
- inhalation of vapours or fumes
- chemical burns
- incompatible substances mixing
- environmental contamination from spills

## REQUIRED CONTROLS
- Check the COSHH assessment and safety data sheet before use
- use the PPE and ventilation specified
- store chemicals in their original, labelled containers
- keep incompatible substances separated

## EMPLOYEES MUST
- Read the COSHH assessment/SDS before using a substance for the first time
- use the specified PPE and ventilation
- store chemicals correctly and keep them labelled
- report spills immediately

## EMPLOYEES MUST NOT
- Use a chemical without checking the COSHH assessment
- decant chemicals into unlabelled containers
- mix chemicals unless specifically instructed to
- dispose of chemicals down drains or in general waste

## GOOD PRACTICE
- Keep only the quantity of chemical needed for the task on site
- check SDS sheets are up to date and accessible
- use spill kits appropriate to the substance

## EMERGENCY / INCIDENT RESPONSE
- Follow the SDS first aid guidance for exposure
- contain spills using a spill kit and prevent them reaching drains
- seek medical attention for significant exposure and report the incident
