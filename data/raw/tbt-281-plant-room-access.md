# TBT-281 Plant Room Access

## Why It Matters
Plant Room Access matters because plant rooms concentrate several hazards — rotating equipment, pressure, refrigerant, electricity and noise — in one space.

## KEY HAZARDS
- Multiple concurrent hazards (electrical, mechanical, refrigerant, noise)
- restricted access/egress
- unfamiliar layout for visitors/new starters
- poor housekeeping increasing risk of other hazards

## REQUIRED CONTROLS
- Complete a plant room induction/orientation before working alone
- keep the plant room tidy and access routes clear
- wear the PPE required for the specific hazards present
- know the location of isolators, alarms and escape routes

## EMPLOYEES MUST
- Familiarise yourself with isolators, alarms and exits before starting work
- keep walkways and access to plant clear
- wear the PPE appropriate to the hazards present
- report faults, alarms or unusual readings immediately

## EMPLOYEES MUST NOT
- Work alone in a high-risk plant room without informing someone
- leave doors propped open unnecessarily
- ignore an active alarm

## GOOD PRACTICE
- Keep plant room housekeeping to a high standard — it supports every other control
- label isolators and controls clearly
- log routine readings so trends can be spotted

## EMERGENCY / INCIDENT RESPONSE
- Follow the plant room emergency procedure for the specific alarm/fault
- evacuate if a refrigerant alarm activates and do not re-enter until cleared
- isolate at the emergency stop if there's immediate danger
