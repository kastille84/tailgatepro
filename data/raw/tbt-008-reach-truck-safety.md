# TBT-008 Reach Truck Safety

## Why It Matters
Reach Truck Safety matters because collisions between people, vehicles and mobile plant continue to cause serious and fatal injuries on site.

## KEY HAZARDS
- Collision between pedestrians and moving vehicles/plant
- poor visibility from the cab
- unauthorised or untrained operation
- overloading
- unstable ground

## REQUIRED CONTROLS
- Keep pedestrians and vehicles/plant segregated
- only operate equipment you are trained and authorised for
- carry out pre-use checks
- use a banksman where visibility is restricted
- observe site speed limits and routes

## EMPLOYEES MUST
- Hold a valid licence/authorisation before operating
- complete pre-use checks and report defects
- wear a seatbelt/restraint where fitted
- give way to pedestrians in shared areas

## EMPLOYEES MUST NOT
- Operate equipment you are not trained or authorised for
- carry passengers unless the equipment is designed for it
- exceed the rated load or working limits
- use a mobile phone while operating

## GOOD PRACTICE
- Sound the horn at blind corners
- keep to designated pedestrian and vehicle routes
- report near misses involving vehicles or plant

## EMERGENCY / INCIDENT RESPONSE
- Stop the vehicle/plant safely and switch off
- make the area safe
- provide first aid if trained and safe to do so
- report the incident immediately

## Supervisor Discussion
- What are the main hazards of Reach Truck Safety on our jobs, and which of today's controls matters most on this site?
- Which of these controls is easiest to skip when we're under time pressure — and what's the cost if it goes wrong?
- What would make you stop the job and ask for help?
