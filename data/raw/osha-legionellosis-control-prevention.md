# Legionellosis Control and Prevention

Water management programs that effectively prevent Legionella growth rely on good system design, proper facility and equipment maintenance, and routine cleaning and disinfection. Keep records of operating procedures, maintenance schedules, inspection dates, and test results.

## Biocide treatment
Halogen oxidizers (certain chlorine and bromine compounds), ozone, peroxides, and non-oxidizing biocides help control Legionella when properly used. Quaternary ammonium compounds commonly used in cooling towers may not fully eliminate biofilm-associated bacteria.

## PPE
Routine maintenance: NIOSH-approved N95 respirators as best practice. Confirmed outbreaks: N100 filters. Also chemical-resistant gloves, eye protection, and clothing appropriate to chemical hazards.

## Cooling towers
High risk; ideal growth temperatures 68-122 F. Install high-efficiency drift eliminators, keep sump temperatures low. Clean and disinfect at least twice yearly (before startup and after seasonal shutdown). Methods: chlorine above 0.5 ppm (pH below 8.0), bromine compounds, non-oxidizing biocides such as fentichlor.

## Domestic hot water
Water heaters kept below 60 C (140 F) risk colonization. Raise tank temperature to at least 70 C (158 F) for 24 hours and flush all outlets for 20 minutes. Alternatives: point-of-use heaters, copper/silver ion treatment, ozonization.

## Domestic cold water
Below 20 C (68 F) generally prevents growth; ice machines and produce misters can run warmer. Hyperchlorination at 20-50 ppm free residual chlorine can address contamination.

## HVAC
Well-maintained systems rarely harbor Legionella; contamination can come from water leaks, poorly maintained humidifiers, or external sources drawn in via fresh-air intakes. Minimize water reservoirs, ensure drainage, maintain regularly.

Mist-generating equipment in grocery stores and dental water lines need weekly disassembly and cleaning with hypochlorite solutions. See CDC, WHO, ASHRAE and CTI guidance; some states (e.g., New York) mandate cooling tower registration and maintenance.
