# TBT-235 Purchasing PPE

## Why It Matters
Purchasing PPE matters because the wrong or poorly maintained PPE fails to protect the wearer at the exact moment it is needed.

## KEY HAZARDS
- Incorrect PPE for the task
- damaged or expired equipment
- poor fit reducing protection
- contamination from unclean PPE
- over-reliance on PPE instead of eliminating the hazard

## REQUIRED CONTROLS
- Select PPE against the task risk assessment
- check condition and expiry before each use
- ensure correct fit and adjustment
- store PPE clean and away from contaminants
- replace damaged items immediately

## EMPLOYEES MUST
- Wear the PPE specified for the task
- inspect it before every use
- report damaged or missing PPE to your supervisor
- store it correctly after use

## EMPLOYEES MUST NOT
- Use PPE that is damaged, out of date or the wrong type for the hazard
- share PPE that is fitted to another person (e.g. RPE, harnesses)
- modify PPE in any way

## GOOD PRACTICE
- Treat PPE as the last line of defence, not the first control
- keep spare PPE on the van
- clean and store PPE properly between jobs

## EMERGENCY / INCIDENT RESPONSE
- Stop work if PPE fails or is unavailable
- make the area safe
- obtain replacement PPE before continuing
- report the failure as a near miss

## Supervisor Discussion
- What are the main hazards of Purchasing PPE on our jobs, and which of today's controls matters most on this site?
- Which of these controls is easiest to skip when we're under time pressure — and what's the cost if it goes wrong?
- What would make you stop the job and ask for help?
