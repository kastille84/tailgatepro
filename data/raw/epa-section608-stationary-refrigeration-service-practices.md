# Stationary Refrigeration Service Practice Requirements

Section 608 of the Clean Air Act requires technicians servicing stationary refrigeration and air-conditioning equipment to follow practices that maximize recovery and recycling of refrigerants (ozone-depleting substances or potent greenhouse gases).

## Evacuation
For appliances with more than five pounds of refrigerant, evacuate to established vacuum levels when opening the system for maintenance, service, repair, or disposal. Levels depend on appliance type and manufacture date (before/after Nov 15, 1993); e.g., high-pressure appliances with 200+ lb require 4 in Hg (pre-1993 equipment) or 10 in Hg (post-1993). Small appliances (5 lb or less): recover 80% or 90% depending on age and compressor function.

## Exceptions
Leaking equipment: isolate leaking components and evacuate them to the lowest level attainable without substantially contaminating the refrigerant.

## Reclamation and oil
Recovered refrigerant staying with the same owner needs no reclamation; ownership transfer requires EPA-certified reclamation. Oil changes must occur at no more than 5 psig so refrigerant is recovered.
