# TBT-253 Abrasive Wheels

## Why It Matters
Abrasive Wheels matters because fixed workshop machinery and cutting tools can cause severe injury in an instant if guards, technique or PPE are not correct.

## KEY HAZARDS
- Contact with blades, wheels or cutting edges
- flying debris/sparks
- entanglement
- kickback from cutting equipment

## REQUIRED CONTROLS
- Only trained, authorised persons use fixed machinery
- check guards and safety devices before each use
- select the correct blade/wheel for the material and machine speed
- wear the eye and hand protection specified

## EMPLOYEES MUST
- Check guards and safety devices are fitted and working before use
- use the correct blade/wheel rated for the machine's speed
- wear the PPE specified for the machine
- isolate the machine before changing blades/wheels or clearing jams

## EMPLOYEES MUST NOT
- Use a machine with a guard removed or defeated
- use a damaged, cracked or incorrect blade/wheel
- reach into or near a running blade/wheel
- leave a running machine unattended

## GOOD PRACTICE
- Keep the machine area clear of offcuts and debris
- check blade/wheel condition before every session, not just at the start of the week
- report unusual noise, vibration or overheating

## EMERGENCY / INCIDENT RESPONSE
- Isolate the machine at the emergency stop immediately
- provide first aid for any injury and report it
- take the machine out of service pending inspection
